Antrum

Antrum is a video project created as a team with Felippe Sousa and Pablo Pereira as part of the Video 2 course in the Multimedia Integration program at Collège Montmorency. It was designed as a stop-motion film created with Dragonframe and edited in DaVinci Studio. At the end of the video, to make our project unique, we added a live-action sequence filmed in a forest, allowing it to stand out. The stop-motion portion of the short film was created in Collège Montmorency’s large studio using movable walls, wallpaper, and various objects that represented a kitchen.

The idea of creating a time loop in the video was relevant to our theme and to the efficiency of the project’s production. We wanted to represent Plato’s Allegory of the Cave in an original way, and we were able to reuse certain sequences several times without having to recreate them.
